## Grep Patterns
- "class.*Controller" in Api/Controllers
- "php-mcp" in composer.json
- "rpc|jsonrpc|method|dispatch" in Jsonrpc.php
- "status" in Tickets/Repositories
- "state" in Projects/Repositories
- "role|permission" in Auth domain
- "dispatch_event|dispatch_filter" across codebase
- "zp_" table name prefix in repositories
- "LEAN_*" in config/sample.env
- "mcp" across codebase (excluding vendor/node_modules)
- "canvasTypes" in canvas repositories
- "kind" in Timesheets service
- "CANVAS_NAME" in canvas controllers

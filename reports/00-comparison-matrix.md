## Bottom Line

The cleanest evidence-backed split is:

- `Leantime` owns the human-facing PM record.
- `Task Orchestrator` owns workflow law, blocker semantics, and next-action computation.
- `Decisions/progress` and `chronicle/history/audit` are intentionally split domains, not single-owner domains, unless a third durable-context system is introduced.

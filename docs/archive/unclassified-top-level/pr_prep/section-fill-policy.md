# Section Fill Policy

## Summary
Must answer: what changed and why it matters.

## Context / Why
Must answer: why this exists and what references matter.

## Verification
Must answer: what has been checked so far, what has not yet been checked, and what remains required. Do not invent verification steps.

## Risks
Must answer: what could break and what surfaces are affected. Must incorporate ambiguity scores if high.

## Rollback
Must answer: how this could be backed out, or explicitly state "Requires maintainer/operator plan" if unknown.

## Reviewer Notes
Must answer: where to focus, what questions remain, and explicitly call out missing docs/changelogs.

## High-Risk Integration Notes (Conditional)
Must answer: why this is risky, what decisions remain, and what further validation will be needed. Only include if risk profile is HIGH or CRITICAL.

# Routing Constraints

1. `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICTING`, or `CLAIMED` authentication and containment facts must
   not map to unattended execution.
2. Mechanical validation is a distinct no-LLM lane and may provide structural
   evidence only within each validator's authority limit.
3. Grok Build, AGY, Gemini CLI, Codex, Claude Code, and OpenCode are candidate
   adapters only. Their plan-backed billing and full containment are unproven.
4. OpenRouter is `API_FALLBACK` and `STATIC_ONLY` for this probe. A future route
   must pin provider/model, price, data policy, retention posture, and fallback
   provenance before any call.
5. The aggregate Dopemux CLI has an observed import-time network attempt. A future
   local broker must avoid that import path for offline audit operations.
6. No final model ranking, broker architecture, or custom runner is selected here.

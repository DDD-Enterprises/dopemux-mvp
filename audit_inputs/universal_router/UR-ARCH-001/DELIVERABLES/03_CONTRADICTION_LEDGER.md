## Handling rule

- **PROPOSED:** Contradictions are preserved as records with evidence, impact, and required resolution. The router must not pick the cleaner story simply because it is easier to encode.
- **PROPOSED:** A contradiction that affects authority, privacy, containment, identity, policy precedence, or release readiness is blocking for the affected route.

## Resolution categories

- **PROPOSED:** `PRESERVE` means the contradiction remains visible and route behavior is conservative.
- **PROPOSED:** `RESOLVE_BEFORE_PHASE` means implementation may proceed only up to the named phase.
- **PROPOSED:** `DEDICATED_PACKET` means no opportunistic repair in Universal Router work.
- **PROPOSED:** `INDEPENDENT_AUDIT` means the architecture auditor must challenge the decision before implementation packet generation.

# RTE Pre-Run Hygiene Stage 4 Challenge

Date: 2026-04-23

## Challenge Summary

The stage-4 question was whether any ambiguous tree should be physically quarantined or deleted before the next RTE run.

## Outcome

- No evidence supported deleting or relocating ambiguous heavy trees.
- The lack of model consensus was treated as a process gap, not as approval.
- The preservation-first fallback remained the safest rule.

## Critical Risk Preserved

Ignoring contradictions is different from preserving them.

This stage preserved:

- split-authority signals
- launch/readiness evidence
- existing proof/report artifacts

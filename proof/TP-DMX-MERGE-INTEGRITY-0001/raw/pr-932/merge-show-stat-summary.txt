    🎨 Palette: Team Dashboard UX & Accessibility Enhancement (#932)

    This PR introduces a series of micro-UX and accessibility enhancements
    to the `TeamDashboard` component, bringing it in line with the
    high-density 'Card-Level Interactive Surface' patterns used elsewhere in
    the Dopemux Ultra UI.

    ### 💡 What:
    - **Interactive Surface**: Made the root `Paper` focusable
    (`tabIndex={0}`) with tactile feedback (hover lift, status-coordinated
    border glow, and shadow).
    - **Header Summary**: Added immediate visual grounding by displaying the
    team's average load and status directly in the panel header.
    - **Consolidated Tooltips**: Moved detailed team insights and average
    load metrics into a single, high-level root Tooltip to reduce visual
    noise and prevent "tooltip fighting" with inner elements.
    - **Stylistic Alignment**: Updated the panel title with `letterSpacing:
    '0.16em'` to match the aesthetic of the `TaskSequencer` and other ritual
    components.

    ### 🎯 Why:
    The Team Signal Board was previously static and required users to scan
    internal metrics to find the aggregate team state. These changes provide
    immediate spatial and temporal grounding, making the team's status
    discoverable at a glance and fully accessible to keyboard/screen reader
    users.

    ### ♿ Accessibility:
    - Added a descriptive, dynamic `aria-label` to the root card.
    - Ensured the root surface is keyboard focusable.
    - Maintained semantic correctness by using status-coordinated colors for
    visual and screen reader feedback.
    - Verified all changes with updated assertions in
    `Accessibility.test.ts`.

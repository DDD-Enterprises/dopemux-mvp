The old acceptance route accepted a signed proof that the canonical schema
rejects. Both paths now agree.

Root cause: the old _validate_embedded_audit was a hand-rolled stdlib check of
`required`, a few enum memberships, and a few types. It never walked `allOf`, so
NO schema conditional was enforced on the signed local-attestation route — not
the exact-model conditional added by this PR, and not the pre-existing SKIPPED
conditionals either (those were caught only incidentally by the separate
passing-verdict policy check). It also exempted `report_path` outright.

Bound: this sits behind the signature trust boundary. It let a TRUSTED signer
declare a forbidden pairing; it did not let an untrusted party in.
